You may cancel your order within 14 days after receipt without giving a reason. Send me an email and return the book within 14 days after that. The cost of returning the book is yours. As soon as I have received the book, I will refund the purchase price (and the standard outbound shipping). The book must be returned in the same condition as you received it.
