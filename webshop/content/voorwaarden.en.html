Payment

You pay securely online via Mollie (including Wero). Your order is final once the payment has succeeded; you will then receive a confirmation by email.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

You may cancel your order within 14 days after receipt without giving a reason. Send us an email and return the book within 14 days after that. The cost of returning the book is yours. As soon as we have received the book, we refund the purchase price (and the standard outbound shipping). The book must be returned in the same condition as you received it.

Condition of the books

We describe the condition of each book as well as we can under "Condition notes". If a book nevertheless differs from the description, please contact us and we will find a solution together.
