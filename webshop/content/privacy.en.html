What data we keep

When you order, we ask for your name, email address and delivery address. We use these to process and ship your order, to send you a confirmation, and to meet our legal record-keeping obligations (bookkeeping).

Payment

Payment is handled by Mollie. We never see your bank details. Mollie processes your data under its own privacy policy.

Cart and cookies

Your cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage) and is not sent to us until you check out. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Sharing and retention

We share your address only with the carrier that delivers your parcel and with Mollie for the payment. We keep orders for as long as the law requires (bookkeeping obligation: 7 years).
