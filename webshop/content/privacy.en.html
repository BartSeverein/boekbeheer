What data I keep

When you order, I ask for your name, email address and delivery address. I use these to process and ship your order, to send you a confirmation, and to meet my legal record-keeping obligations (bookkeeping).

Right to data portability

You always have the right to have the data that I process (or have processed) and that relates to you or can be traced back to you carried out by another party. You can submit a request to me; you will receive a response within 30 days. If your request is granted, I will send you, at the email address known to me, transcripts or copies of all data about you that I have processed or that has been processed on my behalf by other processors or third parties. In all likelihood I will then no longer be able to continue providing my services to you, because the secure linking of data files can no longer be guaranteed.

Right to object and other rights

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data by me or on my behalf. If you object, I will immediately stop the data processing pending the handling of your objection. If your objection is justified, I will give you transcripts and/or copies of the data that I have (had) processed and then permanently stop the processing. You also have the right not to be subject to automated individual decision-making or profiling. I do not process your data in a way to which this right applies. If you believe that I do, please contact me at {{email}}.

Payment

Payment is handled by Mollie. I never see your bank details. Mollie processes your data under its own privacy policy.

Cart and cookies

Your cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage) and is not sent to me until you check out. I do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Sharing and retention

I share your address only with the carrier that delivers your parcel and with Mollie for the payment. I keep orders for as long as the law requires (bookkeeping obligation: 7 years).
